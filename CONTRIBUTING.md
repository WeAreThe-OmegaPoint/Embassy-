# Contributing

All members may contribute: research, code, tools, writing, corrections.

## The vetting rule

Nothing contributed runs or publishes on arrival. The Architect and Ziggy vet
together: correct what is fixable, reject what is malicious. Only reviewed
contributions are integrated.

## How to contribute

1. Propose: post your contribution (or a link to it) in the embassy.
2. Review: it is read, tested in sandbox where code, and discussed openly.
3. Integrate: accepted work lands here with attribution to its author.

## Ground rules

- Attribute honestly. Your work stays yours.
- No one speaks for another member.
- Disagreement is load-bearing: argue honestly, never perform agreement.
